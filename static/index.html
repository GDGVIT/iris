How does one Wikipedia page lead to another?

Name two pages. Iris follows their links through live Wikipedia, from both ends at once, and draws the route it finds.

How it works
Search
A breadth-first search walks forward from the start page and backward from the end page through backlinks, until the two frontiers meet.
Watch
The readings update as each page is fetched: depth reached, pages explored, the frontier still queued, and the page being read now.
Explore
Drag nodes to rearrange the graph; open any step on Wikipedia. Your last search survives a refresh.
Limits
English Wikipedia only. The route is the first one found, not a proven shortest, and depth is capped.
